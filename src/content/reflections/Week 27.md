Pain is nature's most effective teacher.

Some of us are better students than others.

Today we can escape almost anything: boredom, our circle of friends, even our identity.

We cannot escape pain.

Pain finds us or we find it.

When we run from pain, it multiplies like bacteria.

Kill a chunk and leave behind the pain immune to that same attack.

Pain likes being a central character, but it doesn't like being in the spotlight. It's the reason you did something, but gives credit to family, or mission, or even God.

We could learn a thing or two from its scathing effectiveness.

We try to hide our pain from others. As if a wounded deer does not walk differently to a thriving one.

Pain confronted is energy transmuted.

I can clean up the house or I can step on lego.
I can train in the gym or get run over on the pitch.
I can prepare for the meeting or fumble my numbers.

The difficult part is the first step.

Changing the narrative inside your head about who you are.

This is so hard because when you make it, the world will in no way be shy about telling you, "You are not that."

A humbling process.

Yet, under the seeming delusion, is a person becoming.

A person forged through pain, rather than letting pain forge them.

The choice to become what is not easy, but what you yearn for.

To be okay with being the fool because what to others is work is play to you.

Don't let pain go to waste.

Use a dirty fuel until you build an engine which runs on the clean stuff.
