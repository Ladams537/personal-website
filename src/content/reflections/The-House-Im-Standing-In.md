---
title: "The House I'm Standing In"
subtitle: "Week 31"
date: 2026-03-29
---

Speaking to old, but close friends, is like going back to your childhood home: you feel at home, but you know it's a different home from where you now live.

I heard he was expecting child. 

I didn't know how to react. I though this was a room I'd build out before him. 

I felt a flash in the pan of anger and a shower of sadness.

Then joy. Later.

I let myself laugh with him. How crazy this world is!

When getting married, he was terrified of having children. 

How they'd mess up his house and leave toys everywhere. 

Would he be a good dad?  

Would they change him? He kind of liked who he was.

Less than a year later, his wife is expecting. 

He's changed his tune, the room is now painted for them to enter into. 

It's just how I envisioned my same room would look. 

My house does not look like his though.

Colder. 

I don't host get-togethers. I don't have a ritual which holds together what it means to be under my roof. I don't feel the house I built represents me anymore. It was built for another me. 

That terrifies me. 

What if I never find it? 

What if I am never able to build at the rate which matches the rate I change? 

Maybe I need to build out more rooms in my house first; maybe I need to knock down this house and build an entirely new one; maybe, I'm missing the point entirely -- I've been so busy designing the house in my head that I forgot to notice the one I'm standing in is empty. 

I move from house-to-house, it's never quite perfect, so I up and leave. 

It's been easier than dealing with my problems.  

I've recently been sitting in the living room of my problems. 

I have bits and bobs which I can't get rid of collecting dust on the top shelf in my back room where I never go. I keep this room locked. Just a glimpse of these trinkets reminds me of my past errors. Too painful to bear most of the time.

I need to get rid of them. 

I will never need them.

Revisiting them, holding them, *in case I need them*.

I took them down, and the shelf is bare. 

I want to think bare is a problem. A problem I can solve. 

I want to build. Redecorate.

I think I will just sit here for a while.
