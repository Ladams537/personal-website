title: "Renovations"
subtitle: "Week 30"
date: 2026-03-22
---

Hallways of my house.

Some rooms pretty: the physique, the articulation, the close friends; some rooms desolate: the career, the self-love, the procrastination; some rooms delicate: I keep these locked when I can help it. 

Throughout and since university, I've been building extension after extension. 

I *need* a sunroom. I *need* a second kitchen. How about a sauna? 

Building for so long I forgot how to knock rooms down. 

What was left was a beautiful mess only I could understand. 

**Complexity justifying my distance from reality.** 

Many rooms like a jacket from childhood I cherish but don't fit into anymore. 

I saw that I was reminiscing about these jackets too often. 

Wishing I could just slip them on one last time. 

Holding them *in case I need them*. Meanwhile they collected dust. Relics I'd forgotten the names of. 

Each room adding weight to my existence. 

A backpack filled with *maybe I'll need this* and *don't forget about that in case of emergency*.

I was tired. I am still tired. 

Tired of dragging this around with me everywhere. 

Unable to explain what it feels like. 

This week I started renovations. 

I swapped to a building plan of keeping only the rooms I go into every week, letting the others rest in pieces. 

This does hurt. 

Letting go. 

More comfortable to sit in-between choices. To hold onto every room until you can't afford to any longer. 

A way to look back and say, "I tried to win big. Can you blame me?"

You can't enjoy a fancy house when you are worried about paying off its debt. 

The destruction leaves behind debris. 

I am walking around and there's builders everywhere. 

I've had to stop wearing my nice clothes for a while until all this fuss settles down. 

*This sucks!* 

Feels like taking a step back. 

I see though that time was never going to heal me if I continued down my old path; I have to heal myself with time. 

My new house is not always able to produce as much as the old one. 

Not always as grand from the outside. 

However, while my last house was rented, this new one I am able to buy. 

Sometimes I'm bored when the builders aren't around, sometimes I want to run back to what I have known, but like the jackets -- sometimes I just need to leave things in the past where they belong.
