title: "The Weight of Ambition"
subtitle: "Week 23"
date: 2026-02-01
tags: ["Ambition", "Presence"]
---

The weight a man carries is proportionate to the distance between where he is and where he knows he can be.

Ambition is a wormhole.

To live in the wormhole is to exist in a state of perpetual displacement. You are never fully present because you are fuelled by the shadow of who you are becoming.

A life yet to be lived. A version of events believed to complete us.

Whether you find yourself in the 'forever' of youth or the 'counting down' of age, determines the prognosis: nourishment or decay.

Worse: a vacuum of meaning.

This shall be filled. The undirected soul seeks an anaesthetic for the 'now'; the directed soul seeks a tool for the 'next.'

One stays on the planet they know until the resources run dry, the other builds a ship to go through the wormhole.

Creation is hard. Creation is scary. Creation requires a sacrifice to play, a forfeiture of the comfort found in anonymity.

The larger the stakes, the greater the bid. The cost is public failure; the reward is a form of time travel where the imagined 'then' becomes the concrete 'now.'

Yet, there's a special acuity one can grasp when scenarios once dreamed of become reality. It is a sharp, cold clarity. The kind that brings about wistfulness.

The urge to go back through the wormhole—but these collapse behind you. There is only forward, or stillness.

This is the quiet grief in achieving a big goal. It kills the version of you that was still 'becoming.'

The quandary isn't just whether to go through another wormhole, but whether we can survive the stillness between them.

A presence you have become unfamiliar with. A place in space you speak and no sound is made.

And soon that hollow gnawing returns and grasps your attention from the ethereal nebula to a new wormhole.

Maybe Christ said it best in Luke 9:25, "For what is a man profited if he gains the whole world, and loses or forfeits himself?"

There are nebulae all around. But to notice them risks creating another wormhole: the distance between who looks and who doesn't.
