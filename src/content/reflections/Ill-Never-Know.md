I spanned the seas of infinity and mortality; I walked between fertile presence and barren lands of disconnection; I jumped from out of the plane of ego and into here I go again.

All in the same week.

I thought about what it means to live a full life.

About a path set for us in a moment that determines how I spend the majority of our moments.

About chasing the things I want but *know* I shouldn't have and leaving behind the things I *should* be thankful for in their wake.

About looking back and pretending like I can seriously answer the question, "Was it worth it?"

The likely answer is: "I'll never know."
