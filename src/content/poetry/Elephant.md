title: "Elephant"
date: 2026-03-31

excerpt: "On the elephants everyone sees and no one addresses."
---

There's an elephant in the room they tell me.
I see it. I do. Swaying gently, trunk entwined
With another elephant. There's multiple actually.
They're playing with each other,
Grazing branches, squirting water everywhere.
Dare I say the party is over.
After we all recovered from our hangover
No-one could make up their mind as to whether
They were talking about the same elephant.
Jenny protested it was hiding behind Lenny's chair.
Lenny swears he saw it lying down beside Claire.
They did what they did best:
Getting drunk, talking about elephants,
But never daring to actually address them.
