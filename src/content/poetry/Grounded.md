---
title: "Grounded"
date: 2026-03-23
tags: ["Identity", "Loss", "Growth"]
excerpt: "On wings sized for the boy you've outgrown."
---

They told me, “Don’t fly
Too close to the sun.”
I landed —- they smiled.

I slipped my wings off.
Delicately placed them
In the cupboard.

Tomorrow they expect me to fly. 
Pristine wings await me,
But they’re sized for a boy
Who I’ve long outgrown.

I’ve forgotten
What it
Feels like 
To fly
As a boy. 

I’ll never 
Know 
As a man.
