---
title: "A Year On"
date: 2026-03-01
excerpt: "Pain, happiness, and the love that outlasts them."
tags: ["Loss", "Memory", "Romance"]
---

Pain finds me
in moments of memory in-
finite spaces. Time
overcomes these spaces
of me, 
you, 
us, 
them, 
God – 
love.
Pain finds me
welcoming it, to know, it was
real; remind me
I lived it. 
Else, I have 
nothing 
left.

Happiness finds me
when I don’t deserve reprieve
from the silence of suffering.
I shoo it away, usher it
into the corner – where Satan
waits; to
meet me.
Happiness finds me
when I don’t expect it. 
Surprising myself, erasing
patterns – parts of my self, 
I better let 
die
without you.

Love – constant, for-
getting my emotions.
I will always
love
the thought of you, 
and maybe, 
somewhere, 
in the vastness of my soul 
lies the key
you stole to my heart
and within
the fragments –
what’s left:
I have 
the capacity
to always
love 
you.
