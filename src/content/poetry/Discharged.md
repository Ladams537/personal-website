I'd been filing a petition 
for your affection 
for days.
All ignored. 
A normal day, I come home
and I receive a winding-up order.
I show up in the bedroom.

The proceedings accelerate,
sweat decorates my skin:
I prepare for the trial ahead.
I kiss your neck, bite your ear, --
distraction to delay.
You grip my hips, 
slip your hands underneath,
my last layer of protection,
finding my most valuable asset -- 

it feels good to be caught hiding.
*Not sure I'm capable
of handling the upkeep.*
*You had that energy
of someone hiding something.*

I surrender
at the point where ending 
this becomes impossible.
You reassess the quality
of my remaining estate --
one last time --
then adjoin it to yours.
Leading here
I'd leveraged a load of debt -- 
you're left with the consequence.

I am discharged from the bedroom
debts cleared.
A fresh start.
You count up
all the money you now have.
