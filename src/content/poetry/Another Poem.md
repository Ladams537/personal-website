---
title: "Another Poem"
date: 2026-02-12
tags: ["Cathartic", "Suffering", "Hope"]
excerpt: "A bad morning made better."
---

I woke up in a bad mood. Don't know why — 
yesterday was good. The voices spin round in my head, 
the same tired episode of _he said, she said_, 
and I want to get down to work, 
at least I'll forget about it — 
but to run is to let it win.

These mornings are the worst. 
Not because you did anything wrong, 
but because you didn't. 
You sang your song all night long 
and woke up wishing you were gone — 
not _finality_ gone, the kind 
where a life of vitality greets you, 
where people like you for you.

But deep down you know 
if everyone likes you, 
none of them really do.

So you stop. 
The hamster wheel was your own creation — 
motion dressed up as progress, 
spinning dressed up as direction.

Stop trying to stop being you.
Perfection was never the point — 
it was always the mess that moved you.

So when your mind is betraying you, 
when the morning starts wrong 
and you can't find the reason — 
sit down, pull out the pen, 
and write another poem.
