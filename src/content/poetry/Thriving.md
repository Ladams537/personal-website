---
title: "Thriving"
date: 2026-02-27
tags: ["Suffering", "Identity"]
excerpt: "On loneliness dressed as success."
---

Faces of strangers, memories
whisper as one. Lost upstream, eroded by time, 
not fit for this race I am in. I found 
others who can suffer as long as me. 
They are not the same as those 
I left upstream; I miss them; 
now look -- I'm alone.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Surrounded&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;by
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;People
who do not know, who do not share, who do not
understand the rivers from which I came.
But, then, do I?
I needed someone on the journey
with me; and that would be warmer
than any of these *accolades* I have.
I tread, I grasp but the harder I grasp 
the shorter it lasts. 
Powerless; 
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Thriving.
Or so I must believe
if I am to keep on swimming.
