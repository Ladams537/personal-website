---
title: "I See You"
date: 2026-02-14
tags: ["Romance", "Loss", "Love", "Memory"]
excerpt: "Reminiscing, wrestling, ruminating."
---
I see you. 
I can't explain
what I
see. I just know: 
I see you.
Late, graceful, beautiful.
We don't say a word.
Just watch 'The Muppet's 
Christmas Carol' and rhyme
off Christian theology with a bunch 
of zealous, misled people.
You consume me. 
Breaking a heart free 
from my cell. Anxious 
to see you again.
I will for sure.

We landed here. You:me
each other's. Finally.
I see you.
To hold your hand I ask,
because to be bold still
feels like sin.
I still worry lightning 
might strike us both down,
for leaving that place
we met in.
But I don't want to think about that now. 
Your hand I hold in mine. 
Your little black hand fits so neatly
into my sweaty palms
my racing heart
on this silent street 
just outside my house.
I clench a little tighter.
I see you. 
I see my projection
of you. 
But I don't want to think about that now.
I don't want to say it yet, 
but I know I love you. 

I went back to the pier today
where it all ended.
Remembered speaking about this exact 
day and all we would do on it.
Now I sit here without either 
me or you. For those versions of us
died with us that day.
I see you. 
Only in my mind of course.
Sitting beside me
on this bench,
how lonely I can be
while so near your living quarters.
That makes it worse. 
How I know
how close you are 
and yet the scars left do not fade 
from my heart. 
I see you. 
Do you see me? Did you ever
see me or did you continue
like I once did to curate a version of me
that you wanted me to be? 
Or were you too concerned to consider 
that rebels are full of idiosyncrasies.
I see you. 
But, maybe all I see is my reflection of you. 
I spawn you on command, 
or maybe you still pray that you'll come to my mind.
People walk past, new faces,
not to me. They still carry 
the souls that saw me 
crying on this bench.
I can forgive. I cannot forget.
And sometimes, all alone, I wish
I did forget.
I see you. 
I stand, 
and you disappear from view
as I walk home without 
your weight on my shoulder 
for the first time.

	I see you.
