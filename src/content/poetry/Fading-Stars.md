---
title: "Fading Stars"
date: 2026-03-21
tags: ["Beauty", "Observation", "Modernity"]
excerpt: "On what we stop looking up for."
---

The stars, always there 
if I dare look up -- shining.
They just ask I believe in
magic, at least a little.

People used to believe in 
magic, at least a little.
Now, a seven-inch light 
in our palm fixes our gaze.
The stars are still there:

we just never look up.
