Pretty ladies make me so lose it, 
Subjective, at best, … 
God decrees, I flee, back to what I know now,
And will change again when I wake up again. 
We sentence before we listen,
We love and it makes us blind,
We have standards we adhere to, 
We ask others to treat us in kind.
They make us who we are,
But hold others to what we are not. 
They tell us right and wrong,
And come out of us at the wrong time.

It says: she’s a whore, she’s Madonna
One I can’t marry, one I can’t fuck. 
It’s responsible for all this weight I carry, 
But can throw off at any moment.
It’s the reason I say, “fuck me!”
When I know I’m better than that performance. 
For a pretty woman it has pity…
Condemns before you realise, 
Sentences before you speak…
It makes the decisions for us that matter,
Yet we never know the truth under its chatter.
It can help us forge a better life, 
Or condemn us to ridicule and strife.

With age it grows: up, down, around:
Looks in vain at those we respect for it.
Loudest when silent, too much is loud,
If it was a language, we'd all be fluent.
	Makes the decisions for us that most matter,
	Yet we never know the truth under its chatter.

It’s the reason I scream and curse,
When I know I am capable of better.
Condemns the world around all of us,
Sentences whole groups, never plays fair.
	It fools us: our best qualities are innate,
	It teaches: our worst are of parental fate.

Makes me who I am, a quick study,
Pain its potion, an efficient teacher.
I wish it was not as powerful as it be,
Gives me cause to say, 'I'd die for her.'
	For all its flaws I've begun to trust its cryptic voice,
	For more often than not, it makes the truest choice.
