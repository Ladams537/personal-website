---
title: "Beautiful Pigeons"
date: 2026-03-31
tags: ["Comedy", "Romance", "Identity"]
excerpt: "On looking everywhere but where you want to look."
---

I was walking down the street to the gym today.
I saw a beautiful woman walking the other way.
She was dressed like she just came from the gym,
Giddy, walking a little too neatly,
As if she were filing an instagram story,
Wearing her sporty things
I couldn't name if you paid me then.

She looked great; she knew it --
And I felt this irresistible urge to gaze,
But instead I become fascinated
With the pavement.
Lovely pavement it is today isn't it?
A quickening heart greets me,
A smile fades, quickly becoming
A parade of accusations from my brain.

Why you like this?
Speak to her bro.
Boy, Christ is watching.
Future wife incoming.
Relax, you are okay
As those are beautiful pigeons aren't they?

What the fuck is a beautiful pigeon anyway?

I don't know. I will never know.
But in the moment I don't care to know
I just care about not gazing at her.

I walk on, 
I see her walk by
Out of the corner of my eye: 
What the fuck happened to me?
Is it just me?
Is there something wrong with me?
With my body, or, with reality?

I can't comprehend what it's like
To not see everything when I go outside
My house and wander to the gym.
My dad tells me that he didn't really
Know what a woman looked like
Until his first wife.
I found out when I was nine.

My longing for connection,
In the space between
Insecurity and perfection
Is dulled.

I'm not sure I can be fixed.
I have missed so much mystery
In this life I have lived.

Look at that --
The beautiful pigeons led me:
To the gym.
