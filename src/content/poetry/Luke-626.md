Fresh streets, tourism
the smell of success in the air.

They say, you're never
more than six feet 
from a rat.

You learned intimacy 
required you pretend to be
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;clean.
Now, you wish to modernise

and see: 
clean city,
same amount of sewage.
