---
title: "Unwelcome Hope"
date: 2026-07-16
tags: ["Loss", "Hope"]
---

Anaesthetic marinates me with
&emsp;&emsp;moments
&emsp;not contaminated
by this weight.
Spots invaded my forehead,
sleep a break from the poison I consumed: 
unwelcome – hope.

Trees bloom, leaves shed. The earth spins 
inconsiderate of my position on it.
Death brings nutrition; life consumes its victim: 
alone – there is only you.
