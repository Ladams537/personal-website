Loved Chinese, Persian, Nigerian-Antiguan,
A young toy, a soft-hearted bear, a God-fearing Christian.
Chameleons suffer when

Maybe the hole is God-sized, maybe it is just me, 
A life wondering. Disappearing. 

Plans. Their scent. A sundress cigarette 
Summer laid out beside a picnic I didn't make.

Wondering has never warmed you.
