---
title: "Why Haven't You Been Back?"
date: 2026-04-02
tags: ["Romance", "Loss", "Memory", "Longing"]
excerpt: "A walk through streets that belong to someone else now."
---

I thought maybe we could talk.
Retrace all the steps we walked
Before.
Go down the alleyway of
Hey girl, I missed you,
Then turn onto the street
Where we used to take photos
Of me and you.
We'd stand there,
You'd give me your hand, and
I'd hold it,
Not knowing what the plan was next.
But we don't have to go there,
If you don't want to.
Instead,
We could meander through
The memory lane of
Regents Park in golden hour --
Arriving at Primrose Hill
Just in time for sunset.
We wait to see the full moon
Surrounded by people
Smoking weed
Posting instagram stories
Cheering when we make out with each other.
We jump back out at dark
Back into Regents Park
Dancing then doing
Things we're not meant to
In public.

I walk down the high street
Of our memories.
Alone, sometimes.
The lights shine and remind
Me of a time
I was standing beside
You.
The best parts
Litter the streets now.
I'll walk down Kilburn
And see you dancing
In your one bedroom apartment
As an I that I no longer recognise
Knocks on your door.
I'll traipse across to Brondesbury Park
To walk by the flower shop
That I used to go to.
Sometimes I worry they'll recognise me
And ask, "Why haven't you been back?"
Before walking down a long stretch of road
To Wilson House
Where you nursed me back to health
Got sick yourself
And after days of foreplay
We'd go all the way.
Nothing quite hits
Like being nursed from your lowest
And at the end the person is still there.

I still remember your bedroom window
Was two after the right-most
And the night you pulled out the Jenga set
While I ran off to get some chicken nuggets.
Good times;
Too good.
They make
The low, the exhaustion,
Of spending so much time together
Blur: only the most vibrant colours are left.
I make a final stop at my most recent hurt:
Hope Pier.
The place I hoped to hear
That you wanted me
Despite the difficulties we'd ultimately face.
You fought for me
As hard
As I fight
To keep a pair of socks clean.
I could've used a little more good:
In that goodbye.

Maybe I'll see you in a year or two girl.
Chat about our lives.
I'd love that.
I love you
More than the streets
I've just walked through.
No memory can encompass you.
But I think this is the last time
I will come back to these streets
For the very reason they remind me of you.
It's about time I relocated
From West London
To seek a little more good:
And a little less bye.
