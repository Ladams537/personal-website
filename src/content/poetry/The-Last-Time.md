---
title: "The Last Time"
date: 2026-03-19
tags: ["Romance", "Hope", "Loss"]
excerpt: "A farewell that doesn't know itself."
---

I press her soft cheeks 
lightly, but a touch
heavier than the usual,

she smiles quaintly,
biding time to 
let one tear flow
out of her left eye.

Neither of us know.
I braid my hand
through her hair.
Delaying the hug
I already miss.

She squeezes me,
I'd be surprised
by her strength,
but I think
we're drawing
from each other.

I let go 
of the softest
sandpaper I ever
held -- I turn, --
and I wish
I did not look back,
but I could not resist
one last look
at her now full eyes:

this was the loveliest
I will ever know her.
