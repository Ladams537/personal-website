title: "Lads' Night Out"
date: 2026-02-02
tags: ["Friends", "Fun", "Comedy"]
excerpt: "Five limericks, some truth."
---
Two Indian men corner an Irishman in town, 
Tell him they'll all get along just sound. 
He's doubtful at first, 
But they've paid for his thirst, 
So they're in the pub, pints all around.

They vent and rant, 'I can't take it no more!' 
Living my life has become such a chore. 
Inflation’s gone wild,
Dating's been defiled,
But at least we're all gentlemen at our core.

Approach some young lassies who're laughing, 
One winks and says, 'What you boys crafting?' 
They stumble through chat, 
Completely missing the fact,
Her mates roll their eyes at the grafting.

Now arguing who is most pretty, 
They've formed a whole ranking committee. 
The ladies exchange looks, 
Slicker than everyday crooks
And quietly plan their exit from the city.

The women leave them the tab with a wave, 
Find themselves a cab—what a save! 
The Irishman sits, 
Counts coins, has a fit, 
Indian men and pretty ladies will be his grave.
