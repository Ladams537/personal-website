---
title: "Tight Turnovers"
date: 2026-04-01
tags: ["Wit", "Satire", "Modernity", "Comedy"]
excerpt: "RyanAir goes interplanetary. Nothing changes."
---

I dreamed of this as a kid but 
It's not what I expected.
I'm waiting to get on a RyanAir
Interplanetary spaceship.
We're delayed:
A passenger in front of me
Is refusing to pay the charge
For oversized luggage. 

After waiting in the narrow passageway
Until they refuelled the ship 
(Tight turnovers you see), 
I relax into my cryogenic sleep pod
And go into VR. 

Wait, no. 
No, no no! 
Ugh
It is buffering.
The pod beside me 
Is hogging
The bandwidth. 
I look in -- 
This six-foot-two guy 
Is living in the fantasy
That he,
Is a ballerina 
Dating a vampire baddie!
This is what I am giving
Up my bandwidth for? 

I speak to the hostess.
She says, "It will be thirty pence
For a conversation with me." 
I oblige. Scan my wrist over hers
And transfer the money 
Before venting about buddy
Who's hogging my bandwidth. 
She smiles, "Sorry, this is not 
In our customer service policy."
If you'd like to pay-as-you-go
You can pay a thousand pounds 
For one terabyte of data. 
I hold my wrist to hers:
Beeb. 
"Transaction successful, 
You may return to your pod."
I push in the two levers
To release the lid
So I can slide back into
My pod.
I am tired, 
So I breathe
A little too heavily and
Fog up my VR screen.
I wipe it clean, leave skid marks,
And think, 
"I'm never
Flying RyanAir again."
