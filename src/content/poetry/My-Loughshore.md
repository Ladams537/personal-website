---
title: "My Loughshore"
date: 2026-03-17
tags: ["Identity", "Memory", "Home"]
excerpt: "Nostalgia, pride, and a coke can."
---

Dark claws scrape the sky
Tearing the light clouds up.
Cars chuck out junk, driving
To nowhere of importance.

Memories that marinate me now
A short skirt I'm desperate
To just sneak a look at. Proud
I'm not like these pathetic

Losers. I am in no rush.
The scrape of my shoe trailing
The tarmac, scraping dust
Off stone my name should be carved in.

I walk home alone, I throw 
My coke can over the neighbour's
Hedge. "What's the craic 
With those claws in the air?"
