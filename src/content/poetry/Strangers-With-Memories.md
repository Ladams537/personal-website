---
title: "Strangers With Memories"
date: 2026-03-31
tags: ["Memory", "Loss", "Growth", "Community"]
excerpt: "On the people who moulded you before they became strangers again."
---
I've sought sunsets through my life. 
Thinking that if I 
Changed my body, my education, 
My social standing: 
That I'd see them. 
Never changed my perception. 
Not able to see
That those who stood
By me when there was no reason to
Did more for me than my accolades ever would.

Most left.
I mourn them, 
Strangers with memories
Who left me as an angel trapped in stone.
Shouting, screaming, searching.
Their sun sets in a different time zone.
We've outgrown each other, but never know
Which seeds we planted in others will grow
Into trees.

There is no better sunset 
Than the sunset 
You see in hindsight. 
Solving problems instead of being present
Stealing from time its only present: connection. 
These strangers with memories
Are the ones who moulded me 
Making me play the fool 
So that the next room 
I entered into
Those lessons could be my fuel.

Like calling a friend a coward
For starting a fight over text
Instead of waiting for the subtext
Which comes from being next to him.
Like pointing out to my girl
Her imperfections, through my eyes,
Trying to fix her
To mask the pricks of inadequacy
That flickered inside my mind.
But you didn't hear me admit that here.

There are only so many times
You can leave people by the wayside
Before the wayside becomes a graveyard.
Despite their soft words of encouragement
Being there in the loneliness of the night.
Despite telling me I'd be alright
Holding me close, giving up their time.
Despite a sky collapsing into cloud, 
Them at my side brought bursts of sunshine.
I didn't see it. 
Any of it.
In the moment.
I didn't see the stars and the angels 
For I was too busy looking at my feet.
Then they became more strangers with memories. 

I was alone. Truly alone.
In a graveyard of memories.
No-one new in my life could see
The names written on the stones:
Lives I'd lived before,
Strangers I'd once known,
Skins I'd shed by the wayside.
Love. I'd never forget.

I hid from my 
Worst fear: me. 
No me in the gym. 
No me behind my brain. 
No me with company.
Me. Alone, afraid, anxious, 

**Lucid dreams of what I'd do differently**
**If I came across a stranger with memories.**
**Catch-up, renegotiate our status**
**From strangers to strangers who sort of**
**Met up for coffee, just occasionally**
**To reminisce about what we used to be**
**When we were something more** 
**Than strangers with memories.**

Months, no sunset.
Learning to reconnect
With being present 
In a given moment.
Like a Tuesday morning
Walking back from the gym,
Realising my friends
Won't leave me alone 
In my graveyard of memories.
Even if they have to drag me out.
Or the magic 
When the lights go low.
My heart-rate blurs
Drowning in the beat
As I dance like a ballerina
While my friends
Laugh with, or more likely at me.
I could be me wherever I took me.

I crawl, in one direction.
One day I will walk.
One day I will run.
One day I will look 
Back and know what it took
To become
Me.
Not me thinking that I was alone --
Not me longing for someone long gone -- 
Not me wishing to be a someone.

I still go back though…

I still go back to the graveyard on occasion,
Where my memories belong.
I turn on the song, 
Only I can hear
Understanding, I might be 
The last memory 
Standing in this place.
I have passed ghosts, wishing
To be selfish, 
To resurrect them 
To make them flesh. 
They politely decline.
They found another 
To carry them.

A ghost just told me
"Look up, 
Look beyond this place."
I take my eyes off the carved stone,
A skin I shed long ago,
And see that the sunset is there.
Was always there.
I just have to stop looking at my feet.
