---
title: "The Heavy Gold Year"
date: 2025-12-27
tags: ["Memory", "Community", "Burdens", "Resilience", "Growth"]
excerpt: "Recounting 2025 as a year."
---
One little house sits by the sea, 
Rainbow Bay: house 29C.
I walk past, not sure why I'm there, 
All I know is that life ain't always fair.

Cars drive by, each a hidden story, 
Wrapped in a frantic, festive hurry. 
Headphones grip; I dance to the beat, 
Wishing I fit the blueprints: square-edged, discrete.

I tally the months, I watch the tide turn, 
For the ghosts of my past, there's no bridge left to burn. 
Like ships on the horizon, they’ve voyaged away, 
Friendly spirits at sea, in the mist of the bay.

Last year, this walk was hallowed. 
Still. Silent. Celebratory—a new life I was living. 
Now, the salt air tastes like an ending
As I walk the tide-line—
Tight. Taut. Twisted.

A new man doesn't mean a new heart. 
Depth of feeling tears the old one apart. 
I burn inside, a fire unended. 
Overwhelmed by the spirit—
But finally mended.

Twenty-twenty-five: a thrumming, dark light. 
The year of the anvil, the year of the flight. 
I found the deep water by losing the shore, 
A heart made of gold—but tempered by war.

Looking out at a globe where peace might unfurl, 
One day it sure would be nice to raise a little girl. 
And I’ll tell her the truth of this heavy, gold year: 
I was a hero, not a saint; I’ll make that all clear.

I walk back, alone. Through the festive light.
The fire is tempered; the gold is held tight.
No longer a boy, nor a blind optimist,
A skeptic, perhaps—but no cynic exists.
