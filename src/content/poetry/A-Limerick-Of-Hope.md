excerpt: "Five limericks tracing love, faith, and letting go."

There once was a place: ICC, 
Full of people, full of fake glee.
A place with singing and applause,
With scripture and make-belief laws
I joined there to find *me*.

In an angel's wings I was wound,
Dark-skinned Hope, whom I had found.
Beneath her spell, I'd have said 'I do'
But my love for her, she never knew
Until in Paris we were crowned.

Free at last I hopped onto my saddle,
Between heaven and hell I fought my battle.
We quarrelled ceaselessly, 
Made love more relentlessly,
Slowly stacking our tower of Babel.

Word failed us; this ending I did not envision.
Tried to understand her position, her decision.
Which broke me each night anew,
Much I wished I could undo
God can always take what He has given.

Broken beyond breaking, only up now
Fell into a cadence, happier without her.
Though I'll seek her until I die,
Passion and pain never comply,
An Irish Clover which belongs to an English Tulip forever.
