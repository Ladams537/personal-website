Afraid of tall buildings 
because of earthquakes on Instagram.
Afraid of pretty girls 
because of a heartbreak on Netflix.
Afraid of AI 
because of articles on X.

I stepped outside with no phone,
I realised: my bedroom is as scary as it gets.
