---
title: "Taken Off Ice"
date: 2026-03-07
tags: ["Suffering", "Endurance", "Physicality"]
excerpt: "On surrendering to intensity."
---

Sweet presence, timed flaws, my unconscious mind
holds its shape amidst this destabilising force of
your nature, my nature--holding each other while
we teach each other to feel and not think too much.
To stand, to yield, to bear underbelly:
can't think my way through it, can't negotiate my release,
a welcome prisoner: a piece, a pawn, a portrait, a beast.
The predator gets closer
and all I do is wait; for its jaws to open.
Seeing my submission, it bows slow.
I looked at power and it greeted me.

On my back I still lie. Death has departed,
yet I cannot stand. Clawing back a feeling
that pricked my being--reverting the entropy
of done to me with the roar of done by me.
I chase, hungry, becoming something
that is not prey, nor predator, but slave
to the moments before death, when life
presented itself to me, and I was taken off ice.

Buzzing. Convulse, tighten, strained from pulling,
gripping onto this translucent nectar.
My sweat, glitter among the thorns of dirt
scraping into my skin. Gums bleeding,
I have not been hit, bruising though
I am alone with it. River flows over me,
a shell no longer storing an underbelly.

I set out, ravaging outside of my habitat.
Fellow prey, suffering to enjoy suffering,
training to evolve. A twitch in their eye masks
the unhinged nature which propels them on this path.
You know the look. You know what makes us tick,
transmuting rationality for one more hit
that makes claw marks pretty and damage a desirable trait.
Shivering, I cannot move. It's right there,
but I am no longer in my body.
