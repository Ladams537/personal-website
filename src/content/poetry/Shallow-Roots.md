---
title: "Shallow Roots"
date: 2026-04-03
tags: ["Identity", "Connection", "Growth", "Memory"]
excerpt: "On belonging to a place your surface has already left."
---

Smell of lilies and roses.
Budding questions
Fighting for attention.
We are close -- brushing --
Wind sways us above the surface
But the roots are shallow. Damaged
Before, remembering
Signals once sent through the soil.
Uprooted: naked and a changed view.
My surface responds to the environment
While my roots have always had a home.
