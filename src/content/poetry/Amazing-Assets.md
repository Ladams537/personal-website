---
title: "Amazing Assets"
date: 2026-04-01
tags: ["Identity", "Suffering", "Connection", "Friendship"]
excerpt: "On being listed like assets when you are bleeding at the seams."
---

He looked me in the eyes.
He meant it as a compliment
As he said all of it.
Telling me how I
Was such a good friend
And meant the world to him.
He listed off my amazing assets
Like a grocery list:
How I am there for friends when needed.
How I bear suffering beyond
The capacity of a normal human being.
How I learn from my mistakes.
How I possess emotional empathy
Which creates space for both sides
To speak honestly.
How I

Stop.
The poor pen I grabbed
When this tirade began
Has been suffering the fate
Of my hands bending it
To the precipice
Of what it could take.

I return the favour.
Look him in the eyes,
Waver slightly
As I grasp at possibly
The last chance I have
To get my point across
This canyon --

Everything you listed.
Is the problem.
I breathe, a sigh of relief
As I catch my shoulders flying
Leaving my body on the ground.
I set the pen down,
Touch my thigh,
And listen:
My heart is pounding.
My voice trails off over
The edge of the canyon.

I see him. I do.
He sees me too.
He sees the thirty-two-bit version
Of me that's been edited
Through his camera too.
He sees the filters,
My best angles,
But when does this caricature of me
Stop being the actual me?
Did I just learn to dance
In front of his camera lens
So that he would like me?
Have I always done this?
Has he ever seen me?

I pretend to be a little
Worse off; to get him to back off,
To stop taking photos.
Being able to manipulate my emotions,
These virtues he speaks so highly of,
In the moment,
Is like being able to prone
Under the railings,
Entering a *no-go zone*
Without anyone noticing.
He sees my back,
He sees my abilities,
But he doesn't see the scar tissue
Which has accumulated
At the points I am most competent.

I want to go home.
I want to end this discussion.
My skin is crawling.
I feel the heaviness
Hit me all at once.
I stand
Wobbly on my feet
Stumbling to exit.
I feel my heart
Beating through my palm
As I rest it on the doorknob.
I twist my wrist and
I do not turn around.
