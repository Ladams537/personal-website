Fuck. This is the last place
I expected to see my ex -- Hope.
My dreams dilate, I approach
the bench, hoarding starved looks.

Her walk, I have missed,
and those tight dresses
supporting her grace 
full disposition. She turns
just slightly, exactly how
I envisioned her flicker
of insecurity 
and scorn 
would manifest.

I joust forward
toward that curated face.

Hope? 
I whisper,
my throat pummels 
air trapped behind 
my lips.

*What?*
The accent gives it
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;away.
I blush: 

where you from? 
*Ghana.* 

I long to utter
how she's ripped 
me&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;apart.

You look just like her,
*I get that a lot.*

Compelled to show
that she really does
look like her --
to peek a photo 
from files 
which stash 
faded memories of her 

-- nice to meet you --

and, I run:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;breathless.
