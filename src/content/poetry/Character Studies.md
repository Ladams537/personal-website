title: "Character Studies"
date: 2026-02-02
tags: ["Satire", "Mundane", "Wit"]
excerpt: "Satire play on objectification."
---

They hit on me all day long,
Strip me naked, down to my body.
An object: the only group I belong,
With the objection of nobody.

Sometimes I have character on display,
Sometimes they have to give it to me.
Sometimes I sit lonely part of the day.
Sometimes all they do is bang on me.

But I do know my value, a vital part
Of fantasies: without me, not reality.
They tell me everything on their heart,
And I help to give them needed clarity.

I would like to branch out, for sometimes I get bored,
But, hey, I can't complain, it's not a bad life for a keyboard.
