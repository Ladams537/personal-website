---
title: "Last Sibling Standing"
date: 2026-03-25
tags: ["Loss", "Memory", "Faith", "Family"]
excerpt: "The loneliness of outliving everyone who knew you."
---

Tears used to flow. 
I used to weep
Over my brothers and sisters.

*He looked nice didn't he?* Is all
I can bring to the surface.
My grandson despondent;
Lost his grandpa --

His first. 

Lost the last today. 
Solitude the reward
For being the last,
Sibling
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Standing.
My memories are mounted 
On translucent paper.
I've lost my siblings 
With who I could 
Read these cyphers.

God gives, God takes;
Death crawls on my skin.
I count the moments --
Until rekindling
With my codebreaker kin.
