**Monday**
This is unbearable.
I went to the gym soulless. This is not the life I want.

*This feels unbearable.*
*One good day I tell myself. Just one.*

**Tuesday**
I stand where I stood last year. I don't want to tell anyone. But they see right? Can feel their judgement.

*Yesterday wasn't good. But it wasn't terrible.*
*I feel a little bit better.*

**Wednesday**
Couldn't bear to go outside because of yesterday.

*Fell into old habits again.*

**Thursday**
That breathing! The way she talks!
These people don't get it. Don't get me.

*Saw my friends. Made me feel a bit less crazy. I wasn't my best self, but I was there -— at least in body.*

**Friday**
I said gym 3x, read daily, and no alcohol.
I failed at all three.
I'm done.

*Went out to the park for a walk. Kinda feel bad that I didn't get as much work done as I said I would. Didn't even get the chance to train. But, I was greeted by peace. First time in a while. Even if only for a moment.*

**Saturday**
Tried to be happy today. Just made me sad.

*Offered an opportunity to produce a short-film!*

**Sunday**
Yesterday was better than today. Today will be better than tomorrow.

*I saw this guy today. He looked like a mess. I hope he's okay.*
