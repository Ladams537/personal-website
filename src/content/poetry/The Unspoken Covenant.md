---
title: "The Unspoken Covenant"
date: 2026-01-26
tags: ["Free-thinking", "Tribes", "Identity"]
excerpt: "Suppression of thoughts."
---
Told that through my birth, life will be easy,
That all I will ever be, is of race and gender.
Helps the school system killed all creativity,
Or maybe I'd think of a way out of this dilemma.
No winning, lost. Broken and bruised. I stand
A white man consumed by the modern world's stance.

I was primed from a young age: hate is default.
I accepted it, internalised it, defended it.
Performance became my calling card, all my fault
And I gave all of my information to a state
That I believed would solve all my problems,
Not realising: I was seen as one of them.

Think for myself? No. Not yet. Too dangerous -- 
They'll exile me from their intellectual groups.
In time: they're all I have, all I can trust,
And I've lost the man who once had any doubts.
Uncomfortable to never say what I mean: I dispose of it--
Better to think I'm in agreement with the unspoken covenant.

And now I wander, each day: a fresh self-hating onslaught,
But at least I don't know... they're not my own thoughts.
