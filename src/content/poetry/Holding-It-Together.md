---
title: "Holding It Together"
date: 2026-03-25
tags: ["Wit", "Suffering", "Identity"]
excerpt: "On being the piece that holds — until it doesn't."
---

I grow fatter with age. I collect
rust on my soft exterior. No choice 
but to fill the tolerances, affecting
the whole system. My screeching voice
garners no sympathy, no compassion
from my more expensive comrades.
They compress me further, compaction
flakes my coating, my surface fades.
I scream louder each day, begging
for mercy. They won't set me free.
The torque too much, my lubrication
grows thinner. Corrosion snaps me,
precious joints loosen. A tiny bolt
breaks. A whole system comes to a halt.
