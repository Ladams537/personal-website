date: 2026-02-20

---
Good to see you still sit behind 
a screen most days. 
Thought I'd lost you there when
I saw you doing public speaking.
Your smile, your charm, 
this act you put on for the world: 
I can't stand you. 
You're fake bro! 
You aren't me. You broke. You caved.
You let those idiots into your brain 
and now you don't remember
what winning feels like.

*I lost*
*over and over again. *
*I broke. Life broke me. *
*I was so comfortable in that gaming chair,*
*a screen that mirrored freedom,*
*that when it was taken away: I broke.*
*I saw my powerlessness, *
*how despite our superiority and gifts of clarity*
*everything could be taken away.*
*And what was left was stone.*
*The stone you carry in your heart still*
*that feels like safety, but is in fact killing you.*
*A weight you carry, others cannot see, *
*and you are scared to look, *
*because you feel unsteady.*
*You don't know why. You aren't confident *
*in the foundation of your existence*
*and that leaves your life in one place: performance.*
*I shed that skin.*
*This is what came *
*out of it.*

Why do you feel a need
to lecture me on your high horse? 
Bro, you stand there, 
talking about connecting 
with something underneath the surface.
Look at where it got you! Lol!
Look. Come on. You are worse off. 
You feel so much you forgot 
how it feels to not feel.
I oscillate between anger and ecstasy 
with each win and loss, but God knows
I've never had to venture 
into the land of the lonely.
I have my community. I have my recognition.
What about you?

*I stand *
*unknown in this city *
*that makes so many a ghost.*
*An outcast, a rebel: I'm not even fit*
*for the misfit labels.*
*I compete with myself, not with others,*
*a sad excuse to one who's built a life*
*winning in that arena.*
*But I have seen where that road leads.*
*That in competing I am a cog in the machine, *
*losing my autonomy. A slave.*
*A slave with no name, only number.*
*Dissatisfied because there's always *
*a bigger fish to fry.*
*The emotions *
*don't give me the answers,*
*they give me the reason.*

Is that why you go out and create 
different memories each day? 
They don't understand you.
Look at all the effort you've put into being liked!
All the time you spent 'working on yourself'
when there was never anything wrong with you. 
At their beck and call
for their problems. 
Despite all your 'inner work'
you're still scared of removing your armour.
You speak in answers.
A good therapist for others
because all they see is the illusion
of a man.
When you open up finally, 
they run away! 
They run! 
From you.
Too much. Too serious. Too emotional. 
They cast spells on you
and you immediately become
exactly who you just accused me
of being! 
Hypocrite. 
You went into the world and the world said,
"you are not enough, be like us." 
And you caved bro! 
You don't look like me,
don't sound like me, 
don't think like me.
Are you even me anymore? 
Or were you so ashamed of who you were
you had identity surgery of the soul? 

*I tried*
*but I will try again,*
*with more information *
*than the first time.*
*That's all I can ask. *
*To have failed. *
*That is all I have over you. *
*More failures. *
*A trail of them scattered across the path.*
*But each wrong turn, each misclick, *
*is a beautiful scar that adds depth to this broken heart.*
*I know you know what that feels *
*like even though you *
*haven't experienced it yet.*
*Built into us: sensitivity simmers in your bones.*
*You know.*

I... I do envy you in some ways 
you know. Life seems longer 
through your eyes. 
You have friends that check-in
and are in group chats that respond
within hours, not weeks.
You aren't micromanaged by our parents
like I am still, and luckily 
you ran away from this city. 
I've never known a woman,
I've only known videos of them. 
I chat like I've seen the world
but I've only watched it through a screen.
You failed and aren't broken. 
Tried and didn't quit.
Hated what you did
and did it anyway.
Found people who made you care 
more than I ever could.
You adventure, invert narratives
about what you see. 
Take pride in understanding the position
of others not like you.
You've talked to so many cultures 
you've forgotten how mundane yours was,
and choose your own path 
instead of the prescribed one.

*I fell*
*back into old habits through all this.*
*Realised my ability had limits.*
*That is all. *
*Nothing special, no 'quick hacks'.*
*Life humbled me in the way a screen can't.*
*It didn't tell me, "you are not enough."*
*It said, "you already have enough. *
*Stop trying to be and be."*
*In our gaming fortress *
*we could be whoever *
*we wanted to be. *
*Tracer, junkrat, mei, hanzo:*
*we could play all day and reset the next. *
*Mistakes didn't have consequences*
*that we could see. *
*Life humbled me to stop trying to be*
*something more than just a man.*
*And that's what I am. *
*Not immune to mistakes*
*or always self-assured,*
*just a flawed man who puts one foot*
*in front of the other.*

But what I cannot get, is why you feel so much? 
I never cry. I just put my mind
to the task at hand, constantly distracted.
Why choose pain? 
Life is not so serious as you think. 
Be passionate and choose 
what makes itself yours.
At least I have purpose. 
At least I have direction. 
You spend so much time choosing your orientation
you forgot what it's like to be in the arena. 
I don't know what made you so soft. 

*I am*
*who you are.*
*A hard exterior*
*with a soft heart*
*you guard with great care.*
*I need you. Like you need me.*
*The arena is yours.*
*That's your fight.*
*Now let me show you *
*the beauty of a broken heart.*
