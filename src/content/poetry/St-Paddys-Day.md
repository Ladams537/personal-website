Nice to see her. 
We're different, 
but ended up here.

Wait, Samantha? 
Meeting like this 
we got to stop.

Quick, clean, tube 
journey ends any
possibility of awkwardness.

I want to object. 
Not her. Not this. 
But I don't have jurisdiction

and chance isn't so kind 
to an Ulsterman on 
St Paddy's Day.
