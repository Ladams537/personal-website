title: "Baptism"
date: 2026-10-05
tags: ["Identity", "Growth"]

I stand at my altar
Filled with dirt 
And freezing cold water. 
As I am here, I won-
Daring, “When I go under, 
And go before my Maker,
Who, or maybe 
Just, will I 
Become?” 

I watch at my precipice
As my vellus hairs legionnaires 
Hear the command,
“Do not turn away lads!”
As shards of ice, they hold 
The line and watch as – 
One plunges; 
Cheers! All around
Eyes fixed.
A death – a life, 
Better?
Two plunges;
More cheers
And now, it is I who
Must go forth: I am held 
By those who chartered me here.
Trusted: some are; some not.
I feel a heart grow and room is shaped
From the fear of us both.
My two foot are sacrificed, first,  
Then calves, quads, and soon,
As I am ripped in two, 
As the anxieties and rebellions 
Of old brought me 
To this alter,
As I cling to the last-
In’ parts of me – E-
Go under.

I emerge,
London Bridge above,
With a decision
I can't reverse.
