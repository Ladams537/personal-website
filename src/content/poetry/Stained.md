title: "Stained"
date: 2026-03-22
tags: ["Observation", "Memory", "Solitude"]
excerpt: "Morning rituals and the marks they leave."
---

Winnie the pooh dancing
faster with each coffee, each day.

My finger applies pressure,
the mug tilts, my eyes meet
the coffee ring I cannot erase.

"See the Good in All Things"
the mug reminds me -- 
surfacing droughts:
mornings where this mug
was my only safe place.

As the liquid drips onto my tongue,
relief: warm, nutty, citrus coffee.
Tingling face, pleasant spinal shiver,
distracting me --
he wears his wounds proudly.
