title: "Trees"
date: 2026-02-01
tags: ["Community", "Loneliness", "Connection"]
excerpt: "Loneliness in a connected world."
---
Trees mock us. We see them spread across the hill
and take them for strangers, ordinary, apart—
but underground they share a single will,
each root sutured to another's heart.
  
We are not so generous. We perform
the version of ourselves most likely to be kept.
The mask fits so precisely, fits so warm,
we forget the face beneath has wept.

But sometimes time kneels down. A stranger speaks
and something in the woodwork of your chest
swings open—hinge you didn't know, door that creaks—
and you stand there, undone and underdressed.

So now I walk out daily, looking for
the one whose ego doesn't bolt the door.
