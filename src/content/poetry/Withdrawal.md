---
title: "Withdrawal"
date: 2026-03-09
tags: ["Addiction", "Suffering", "Desire"]
excerpt: "Meeting euphoria again."
---
Sweet presence, timed flaws, my unconscious mind, 
Holds it shape amidst this destabilising force of
Your nature, my nature--holding each other while
We teach, each other to feel and not think too much.
Best spoken about, better left unperformed,
A caster of compulsion consuming order, producing 
Chaos. To stand, to yield, to bear underbelly:
Fear and excitement, a sign you're in the middle of it.
Can't think my way through it, can't negotiate my release,
A welcome prisoner: a piece, a pawn, a portrait, a beast.

Control: comfort lurks in its murky waters,
Exposing me. I shrivel, contract back to my old self,
Temporarily as the predator gets closer
And all I do is wait; for its jaws to open. Not long left.
But, seeing my submission, it bows slow,
Still I cannot move--shock subsumes action,
Placing anxiety in its place. A frame
Lengthens into a scene, expanding satisfaction
To meet intensity. Can this be? Today,
I looked at power and it greeted me.

On my back I still lie. Death has departed,
Yet I cannot stand. Weak at my knees, primal instincts
Resisting this transition back to normalcy.
I'm stuck, here. Clawing back, a feeling that pricked
My being--reverting the entropy of done to me
With the roar of done by me. I chase, hungry,
Becoming something that is not prey, nor predator
But is a slave to the adrenaline of the ecstasy
I felt in the moments before death, when life
Presented itself to me, and I was taken off ice.

Buzzing, clinging to my previous state,
I cannot come down. I fear I might lost it.
Can I can lose it? Is it a part
Of me, or is my memory taunting reality?
Convulse, tighten, strained from pulling
Gripping onto this translucent nectar.
My sweat, glitter among the thorns of dirt
Scraping into my skin from that experience
That position of absolute acceptance of
My vulnerability: tormenting me with what was.

Grip grows weary of this power it clings to,
Power I blockade in this arena with me
Fighting to the death, not letting it leave.
Gums bleeding, I have not been hit,
Bruising though I am alone with it,
Fatigue at wrestling with myself. Lying
Down, river flows over me, I am unfit
To hold onto, to retain such euphoria,--
A shell no longer storing an underbelly.

My face feels like a line, a singular vein
Tracing down, through my whole body, beating
Violently now. An animal once tame
Now thinks, does not feel, I am alive.
This land, no source, not enough energy to drip
Into my system and make me reclaim existence.
I set out, ravaging outside of my habitat
I see fellow prey, suffering to enjoy suffering,
Training to evolve. A twitch in their eye masks
The unhinged nature which propels them on this path.

You know the look. You know what makes us tick,
Transmuting capability and rationality for one more hit
Of that intensity, that weightless moment that
Makes claw marks pretty and damage a desirable trait.
To feel, exhilarating; to hold, draining; to rediscover;
Indescribable feeling bleeding from pain and passion in collaboration. 
To dream, but never have believed, we are met with it.
What we sought. There. In front of us the sum of our past rumination.
Shivering, I cannot move. It's right there, the feeling,
The predator of my imagination, but, I am no longer in my body.
