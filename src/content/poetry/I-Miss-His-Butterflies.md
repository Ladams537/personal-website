---
title: "I Miss His Butterflies"
date: 2026-10-26
tags: ["Love", "Memory"]
tone: ["Longing"]
---

One hundred kilobytes of data captured
On camera. I expand. Your eyes shine,
Your whole body lifts, about to take off
Toward a man no longer around to see this.

Your gaze rests, fixed upon his lens.
You hold the flowers tight, cheeks waiting
For his soft (sweaty) palms. Before his palms,
His voice: the notes you played until you fell.

Now, just one finger

Traces your jawline. The dry tip drags
Past your chin and keeps going, down the cold glass,
Until you shrink to a thumbnail
And all that is left is my reflection.
