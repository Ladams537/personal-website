It's a nice thing to fall into a rhythm in life, to be a passenger in my own life, and then still be able to surprise myself and wake up from the dream of it all. Sadly, bubbles aren't burst with laughter. They burst under the weight of tears. Pain. Often we let this pain go to waste: we hide it, we numb out. I got myself involved in some crazy things, coupled with my final year of uni, and changing as a person to understand the world around me more. I'm not sure if a person can be poison dressed as poetry and also be the person who turns a drought into a blossom, but, if they can, maybe that juxtaposition, is who I was, and who I was, tells you more about where I was, than I can put into words myself.
