---
title: "What doesn't change"
---

I adapt quickly, sometimes too quickly, to wherever I am. So rather than a fixed description, here's what has stayed true through every version of me:

I'm drawn to the parts of us we hide. To the parts of people and things we don't feel are worthy of showing to others. Maybe I am just hoping one day we will all have a slightly larger emotional Overton window. Maybe, if I stop putting on an act, I may just begin to live.
