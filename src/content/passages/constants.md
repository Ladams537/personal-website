I adapt quickly, sometimes too quickly, to wherever I am. So rather than a fixed description, here's what has stayed true through every version of me:

I want people to get out of their heads and feel something. I'm drawn to the hidden part of things: why a check failed, why a feeling won't settle, what someone could do if they got out of their own way. And I'd rather be in a room with people than behind a screen.
